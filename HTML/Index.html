PARAGRAPH

To write a truly effective and engaging paragraph, one must consider not just the length, but the coherence and depth of the content. A long paragraph should develop a single, unified idea, using a clear topic sentence to introduce the main point. Subsequent sentences should offer supporting details, compelling evidence, or illustrative examples that build upon that central idea, ensuring a smooth and logical transition from one thought to the next.
